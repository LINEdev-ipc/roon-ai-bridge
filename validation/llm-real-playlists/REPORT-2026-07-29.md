## Confirmed defect 1: combined MusicBrainz artist credits

RoonIA queried `artistname` with the complete model-provided credit and then
compared that combined string only with individual MusicBrainz artists.
MusicBrainz documents `artist` as the combined recording credit (including
join phrases) and `artistname` as an individual artist name:

- <https://musicbrainz.org/doc/MusicBrainz_API/Search#Recording>
- <https://musicbrainz.org/doc/Artist_Credits>

The implementation now:

- searches the official combined `artist` field;
- reconstructs MusicBrainz credited names with their join phrases;
- accepts either the intended individual artist or the verified combined
  credit.

Focused validation: 30/30 recording metadata tests pass, including a new
three-artist collaboration regression.

## Confirmed defect 2: Roon revalidating featured credits

After MusicBrainz verified a complete collaboration, RoonIA still required
Roon to expose every featured artist in the same shape. Roon frequently exposes
only a lead performer for an otherwise correct playable track.

The binding candidate now requires:

- the MusicBrainz-verified canonical title;
- the canonical lead artist in Roon;
- the existing version and recording-evidence gates;
- any performance-sensitive roles such as conductor, orchestra, ensemble or
  soloist.

Featured-credit truth remains in MusicBrainz metadata and is no longer
re-proven from Roon. A focused regression verifies a three-artist MusicBrainz
recording against a Roon result containing only the lead artist.
