# v0.20.0 Beta 12 Exact Recording Families Validation

## Why this beta exists

Beta 11 fixed the ordinary playlist path but the complete real-Roon suite saved
only 803 of 1,000 requested tracks. Remixes reached 43%, live recordings 73%,
the acoustic/cover build rejected all 160 candidates, and the constrained
classics list reached 87%.

## Corrective design

MusicBrainz remains the source of recording identity and metadata:

- published `mix`, `remix`, `rework`, `remodel` and `rewound` labels are
  recognized without confusing original mono/stereo mastering mixes;
- `dub` and `acoustic` are represented explicitly instead of being collapsed
  into generic alternate recordings;
- release titles participate in version evidence, allowing albums such as
  `MTV Unplugged` and named live releases to distinguish a performance whose
  track title is otherwise identical to the studio recording;
- the recording-resolution cache namespace advances to `v7` so earlier
  negative or ambiguous decisions cannot mask the corrected algorithm.

Roon remains only the playback binding:

- cover intent accepts the standard recording by the named covering artist;
- live and acoustic recordings may bind a normal visible title when the
  observed Roon album matches the supplied live/acoustic album evidence;
- Roon discovery uses studio ranking for covers and acoustic performances and
  remix ranking for dub versions;
- candidates that initially lack version metadata may be hydrated before the
  strict version gate is applied.

The MCP instructions now require the published version title and album for
remix, live and acoustic candidates. Covers use the covering artist and its
album without inventing a title suffix. Constrained lists require 150% of the
target; P09 is correctly classified as constrained.

An all-rejected diagnostic build now retains the complete rejected-candidate
and timing evidence while the normal MCP error remains bounded to 25 visible
candidates.

## Incremental corpus

Fixture generation keeps the same ten human prompts and original candidate
source corpus. It contains:

- 125 candidates for P01;
- 150 candidates for P02-P05, P09 and P10;
- 160 exact-family candidates for P06-P08;
- no embedded MusicBrainz recording IDs.

The candidate corpus remains incremental. Only pool classification and
recording semantics change.

The generated corpus contains 1,505 candidates in total.

## Validation order

Before deployment, the complete automated suite, TypeScript build, fixture
generation and intent audit must pass.

Result on 2026-07-28:

- 301 tests passed, 0 failed;
- TypeScript build passed;
- fixture generation produced 10 playlists and 1,505 candidates;
- ordinary and constrained prompts contain only standard recording intent;
- exact-family prompts contain only remix, live, acoustic or cover intent;
- no fixture candidate contains an embedded MusicBrainz recording ID.

After deployment, run the previously failing P06-P09 cases first with
diagnostics and no playback. If all four reach 100 tracks without version
regressions, rerun P01-P10 in full. No validation operation starts playback or
changes a Roon zone.

## Deployed beta 12 result

The deployed commit `aa696c9` reported version `0.20.0-beta.12`, a healthy
container and ready Roon core, transport, browse and image services. The
isolated P06-P09 run used the real MusicBrainz, ListenBrainz and Roon binding
services without starting playback.

| Case | Intent | Beta 11 | Beta 12 | Beta 12 elapsed |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| P06 | Remixes and extended versions | 43/100 | 65/100 | 298,910 ms |
| P07 | Live recordings | 73/100 | 71/100 | 275,168 ms |
| P08 | Acoustic recordings and covers | 0/100 | 65/100 | 248,466 ms |
| P09 | Constrained 60s-80s classics | 87/100 | 100/100 | 315,424 ms |

Beta 12 accepted 301 of 400 requested tracks from 629 processed candidates.
It made 784 MusicBrainz requests, reused 1,139 ListenBrainz cache observations,
performed 1,005 Roon searches and created 301 bindings. The four measured build
times totalled 1,137,968 ms.

The 328 rejected candidates break down as:

- 156 Roon binding failures;
- 73 MusicBrainz not-found results;
- 68 MusicBrainz ambiguities;
- 28 MusicBrainz provider errors;
- 3 duplicate proposals.

The dominant Roon subreason was low identity confidence (97 candidates).
ListenBrainz was warm, but the MusicBrainz recording cache was cold because of
the deliberate `v7` namespace change.

P08 exposes a remaining semantic defect: all 65 accepted tracks are covers.
None of the 71 acoustic candidates was accepted. The acoustic candidates
account for 41 Roon binding failures, 25 MusicBrainz not-found results, three
provider errors, one ambiguity and one duplicate.

P07 also contains a version regression. Candidate P07-019 requested Nina
Simone's `The Other Woman` from `Nina Simone at Town Hall` (1959), but the
automatic binding accepted a distinct live recording from 1985 on
`Live & Kickin (Vol. 1)`. A live album is therefore being treated as sufficient
evidence even when it is not the requested performance.

The live MCP endpoint was checked directly. It exposes server version
`0.20.0-beta.12`, 41 tools and the intended one-shot playlist instructions:
125% reserves for standard lists, 150% for constrained lists, 160% for exact
recording families, and verified partial persistence with explicit added and
missing counts.

The P06-P09 gate failed, so P01-P10 was intentionally not rerun. Beta 12 is not
a release candidate for the exact recording-family workflow.
